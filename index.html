LEARN TO RECOGNISE SCAMS

Scam Awareness Library

Simple examples of common scams and the safest next step.

💸

UPI payment scam

A stranger says you must enter your UPI PIN to receive money. You do not need a UPI PIN to receive money.

🏦

Fake KYC warning

A message threatens to block your account unless you open a link. Contact your bank using its official app or number.

🎁

Lottery or prize scam

A caller says you won a prize but must pay a fee first. Do not pay to claim an unexpected prize.

💼

Fake job offer

A recruiter asks for money or sensitive documents before a verified offer. Check the company independently.

🔗

Phishing link

A link imitates a trusted website and asks you to sign in. Open the official app or type the known website address yourself.

📱

Remote access trick

Someone asks you to install an app or share your screen to “fix” a payment. Do not give unknown people access to your device.

STEP-BY-STEP SUPPORT

Emergency Help Guide

If you think you have been scammed, stay calm. Use these general steps and seek help from a trusted adult or official service.

Call 1930 in India
1. Stop the interaction

Stop replying, stop payments and do not share any more information. Do not follow instructions from the suspicious caller.

2. Contact your bank or payment provider

Use the official app, website or number printed on your card. Ask them to secure your account or payment method.

3. Report financial cyber fraud

In India, call 1930 as soon as possible for cyber financial fraud and visit cybercrime.gov.in for the official reporting process.

4. Keep evidence safely

Keep transaction references and message screenshots. Do not post private details publicly. Ask a trusted adult to help.

This guide is educational and is not a substitute for advice from your bank, police or an official authority.

REMEMBER THESE RULES

Digital Safety: Do's and Don'ts

Simple rules for everyday phone, social media and payment use.

✓ Do

  • Use a different password for important accounts.
  • Verify payment requests with the person through another channel.
  • Install apps from official app stores.
  • Ask a trusted person if a message feels confusing or urgent.

✕ Don't

  • Never share OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords.
  • Do not scan an unknown QR code to receive money.
  • Do not install remote-access apps at a stranger's request.
  • Do not forward unverified offers or warnings.
